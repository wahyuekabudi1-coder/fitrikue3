## Brand & Style

### Brand Personality & Philosophy
This design system embodies the warmth, sensory indulgence, and artisanal mastery of bespoke pastry craftsmanship. It combines the tactile intimacy of a boutique bakery atelier with the pristine clarity of a premium digital gifting experience. The visual language evokes an immediate feeling of comfort, celebratory joy, and meticulous attention to detail.

### Target Audience
- Discerning urban consumers seeking celebration cakes, wedding tiers, and customized artisan pastries.
- Gift-givers requiring dependable scheduling, lead-time transparency, and seamless messaging integration (such as WhatsApp ordering confirmations).
- Corporate and event planners looking for high-tier aesthetic presentation and customized volume baking.

### Aesthetic Movement
**Tactile Warm Modernism with Editorial Editorial Elegance**. 
The system bridges classic European editorial typography with modern, clean e-commerce utility. Instead of cold white backdrops, the environment relies on warm culinary neutrals reminiscent of clotted cream, stone counters, and toasted cacao. Visual structure remains strictly disciplined, preventing decorative clutter while elevating food photography to hero status.

## Colors

### Palette Architecture
The palette is rooted in culinary associations: rich berries, dark single-origin chocolate, and fresh churned cream.

- **Primary (`#C62828` - Ruby Red):** Expresses celebration, appetite stimulation, high-priority interactions, active states, and commercial conversion moments. Never overused on large surface fills; preserved for deliberate focal anchors.
- **Secondary (`#3B2925` - Deep Cacao):** Replaces harsh standard blacks. Serves as the bedrock for all headline and body typography, borders needing visual weight, and secondary button frames. Conveys quiet craftsmanship and rich grounding.
- **Tertiary (`#ED6C02` - Warm Amber):** Highlights urgency, slot allocation, and pending production states (e.g., "Sisa 2 Slot Pre-Order").
- **Neutral Base (`#FFF5E9` - Warm Cream):** The global canvas tone. Emits radiant warmth, reduces optical glare, and provides an organic backdrop that complements warm pastry tones.
- **Neutral Elevate (`#FFFFFF` - Pure White):** Used strictly for interactive container cards, modals, and product showcases to detach them cleanly from the canvas.
- **Structural Outlines (`#EADBC8` & `#E2D3BE` - Warm Sand):** Low-contrast dividers and card perimeters ensuring tactile definition without clinical gray lines.
- **Supportive Accents:**
  - Soft Tint (`#FEE8E6`): Subtle ruby blush for pre-order pill tags, badge fills, and celebratory item backgrounds.
  - Success (`#2E7D32`): Verification confirmation, order validation, and WhatsApp status indicators.

## Typography

### Structural Pairings
- **Headlines & Feature Titles (`Playfair Display`):** Delivers an editorial, human, and bespoke artisanal identity. High contrast serifs highlight product titles, emotional quotes, and hero taglines. It balances the artisanal craft of French/European baking traditions with modern restraint.
- **Functional Interface & Data (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Ensures pristine readability for production dates, price tags (Rupiah currency amounts), specification metrics (e.g., diameter, tiers, allergen warnings), and date selectors.

### Editorial Hierarchy Rules
- Never use italic styling on sans-serif UI elements. Reserve subtle italics for `Playfair Display` decorative adjectives within hero sections (e.g., *"Artisanal* Celebration Cakes").
- Numbers displaying prices must always employ proportional lining figures with bold sans-serif weight (`Plus Jakarta Sans` 600 or 700) to ensure immediate scanning comprehension during checkout.

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid Philosophy
The layout relies on a 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (max width: `1280px`), transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablet devices, and a 4-column layout on mobile devices. Generous white space prevents the digital storefront from feeling like a high-density discount supermarket, reinforcing a luxury boutique atmosphere.

### Breakpoints & Adaptations
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px):** 12 columns, 24px gutters, minimum 48px outer margins. Products are laid out in 3-column or 4-column cards. The order flow and cake customizer feature a 7:5 ratio between preview and configuration panels.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, 20px gutters, 32px outer margins. Catalog shifts to 2-column cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 20px outer margins. Cards collapse into single-column showcases or horizontally swipeable artisan galleries. Critical interactive elements (like the date picker and WhatsApp consultation button) attach to a fixed bottom drawer.

## Elevation & Depth

### Ambient Cream Atmosphere
Elevation avoids cold, grey drop shadows. All ambient shadows are infused with the warmth of cacao and warm amber, mirroring how natural daylight interacts with stone bakery surfaces.

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Pure Warm Cream (`#FFF5E9`). Used for structural backgrounds, full-bleed hero zones, and secondary content blocks.
- **Level 1 (Resting Cards & Panels):** White surface (`#FFFFFF`) with a warm, low-intensity boundary:
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(59, 41, 37, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(59, 41, 37, 0.02)`
  - Outlined with a 1px border of `#EADBC8`.
- **Level 2 (Hovered Cards & Dynamic Menus):**
  - `box-shadow: 0 12px 28px -4px rgba(59, 41, 37, 0.08), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(59, 41, 37, 0.03)`
  - Translates the card upward by -2px to simulate gentle physical lift.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Date-Picker Flyouts, Shopping Cart Drawer):**
  - `box-shadow: 0 24px 48px -12px rgba(59, 41, 37, 0.16)`
  - Accompanied by a tinted backdrop overlay of `rgba(59, 41, 37, 0.45)` with a 4px backdrop blur.

## Shapes

### Contour Strategy
The roundedness level is balanced between structured luxury and soft, dough-like approachability:
- **Base Components (Inputs, Selectors, Panels):** 8px (`0.5rem`) to maintain structural precision.
- **Cards and Dialog Containers (`rounded-2xl`):** 16px (`1rem`) to 20px (`1.25rem`), reflecting the soft corners of pastry boxes and cake boards.
- **Action Pills and Status Tags (`rounded-full`):** 9999px for interactive buttons, badge chips, and lead-time reminders, providing an organic, tactile aesthetic that invites touch.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Ruby Red):**
  - Surface: `#C62828`, Text: `#FFFFFF`, Border: None.
  - Shape: Fully rounded (`rounded-full`) or `rounded-xl` with generous horizontal padding (`px-6 py-3.5`).
  - Hover state: Brightness shifts to `#B71C1C` accompanied by a subtle warm glow (`0 4px 14px rgba(198, 40, 40, 0.25)`).
- **Secondary Action (Chocolate Outline):**
  - Surface: Transparent or `#FFFFFF`, Border: 1.5px solid `#3B2925`, Text: `#3B2925`.
  - Hover state: Surface shifts to `#3B2925`, Text transitions to `#FFF5E9`.
- **WhatsApp Consultative Button:**
  - Surface: Deep Forest Emerald (`#2E7D32`), Text: `#FFFFFF`, with chat icon integration. Reserved for bespoke design assistance and rapid confirmation.

### Product & Artisan Cards
- Background: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`), Border: 1px solid `#EADBC8`, Radius: 16px (`rounded-2xl`).
- Image Area: Aspect ratio 4:5 or 1:1, overflowing cleanly to the card edges at the top, equipped with an automatic badge overlay top-left.
- Content Stack:
  - Lead-time badge directly above title (e.g., "PO Min. H-3").
  - Title set in `Playfair Display` (`headline-sm`), Color `#3B2925`.
  - Flavour profile notes in `body-sm`, muted tone.
  - Price displayed prominently in `title-md` (`Plus Jakarta Sans`), right-aligned or paired with a quick-add pill button.

### Badges & Lead-Time Pills
- **Pre-Order Slot Pill:**
  - Background: `#FEE8E6`, Text: `#C62828`, 1px solid `rgba(198, 40, 40, 0.2)`. Fully rounded.
- **Slot Capacity Warning:**
  - Background: `#FFF3E0`, Text: `#ED6C02`, 1px solid `rgba(237, 108, 2, 0.2)`.
- **Category Badge:**
  - Background: `#FFF5E9`, Text: `#3B2925`, 1px solid `#E2D3BE`.

### Form Fields & Pre-Order Date Pickers
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`, Border: 1.5px solid `#EADBC8`, Text: `#3B2925`, Radius: 10px.
- Focus State: Border color transitions immediately to `#C62828` with an ambient focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(198, 40, 40, 0.12)`).
- Custom Slot Calendar:
  - Fully booked dates are marked with muted strikethroughs on `#FFF5E9`.
  - Selected delivery date features a solid `#C62828` fill with pure white typography.

### Header & Navigation
- State: Fixed/Sticky with a blurred translucent backing (`rgba(255, 245, 233, 0.92)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`).
- Border: 1px bottom border in `#EADBC8`.
- Content Layout:
  - Left: Navigation links (Menu, Custom Cake, About Atelier, Pre-Order Schedule).
  - Center: Brand wordmark in refined `Playfair Display`.
  - Right: Search drawer trigger, Order Status tracker, Cart Icon with Ruby Red counter chip, and Primary "Pesan Sekarang" CTA pill.
